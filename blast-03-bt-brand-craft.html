Tennessee Small Batch Bourbon

Rooted in Tennessee.
Refined by Time.

“We don't rush whiskey. We let Tennessee do what Tennessee does best.”

Every bottle of Bib & Tucker starts with a promise: source the finest grains, trust the process, and let the barrel do the talking. Our small-batch bourbon is aged in hand-selected American white oak, deep in the hills of Tennessee, where the climate and the craft come together in every sip.

From grain to glass, we believe great bourbon isn't made — it's earned. That's the kind of patience you can taste.
