Our Story

Rooted in Tennessee.
Refined by time.

“Small batch Tennessee bourbon. Six years in American white oak, filtered through sugar maple charcoal — the Lincoln County Process that defines the house style.”

Every bottle of Bib & Tucker begins with a conviction: that great bourbon can’t be manufactured, only earned. We source the finest grains, char our barrels by hand, and filter through sugar maple charcoal — the same Lincoln County Process that has defined Tennessee whiskey for generations.

From grain to glass, patience is our most important ingredient. At 92 proof, you can taste every day of those six years.
