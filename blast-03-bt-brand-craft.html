Our Story

Rooted in Tennessee.
Refined by Time.

“We don’t rush whiskey. We let Tennessee do what Tennessee does best — make something worth waiting for.”

Every bottle of Bib & Tucker begins with a conviction: that great bourbon can’t be manufactured, only earned. We source the finest grains, char our barrels by hand, and let time do the rest — deep in the hills where the humidity, the oak, and the seasons conspire to create something no distiller could achieve alone.

From grain to glass, patience is our most important ingredient. And after six years, you can taste every day of it.
