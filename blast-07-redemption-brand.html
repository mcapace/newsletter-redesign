The Rye Revival

Rye whiskey built America's cocktail culture. Then it nearly disappeared. Redemption brought it back — bold, unapologetic, and built to mix.

Before bourbon dominated every back bar, rye was the backbone of the American whiskey tradition. The Manhattan. The Sazerac. The Old Fashioned. All born from rye's distinctive spice and complexity.

Redemption was founded on a simple belief: that rye deserves its place at the table again. Our high-rye mash bills deliver the character, the bite, and the depth that made American whiskey legendary in the first place.
