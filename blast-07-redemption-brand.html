America’s Original Spirit

The Rye
Revival

Pre-Prohibition rye revival. Rye whiskey built America’s cocktail culture. Then it nearly disappeared. We brought it back — bold, unapologetic, and built to mix.

Before bourbon took over every back bar in America, rye was the backbone of the whiskey tradition. The Manhattan. The Sazerac. The Old Fashioned. All born from rye’s distinctive bite, its spice, its complexity.

Redemption was built on a single conviction: that rye deserves its seat at the table again. From our 95% rye mash to our high-rye bourbon, every expression delivers the character, the edge, and the depth that made American whiskey legendary in the first place.
