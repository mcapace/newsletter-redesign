Wine of the Week
91

Quinta do Regueiro Alvarinho Vinho Verde Monção e Melgaço Foral de Melgaço Old Vines 2024

$20 · 12,000 cases made · 7,000 cases imported

A mouthwatering white, showing an expressive mix of ruby red grapefruit, lime zest, nectarine and green almond notes. This is lightly fleshy, with pleasing plumpness to the juicy fruit flavors, while a tang of salinity refreshes the finish. Drink now. From Portugal.

— Alison Napjus
